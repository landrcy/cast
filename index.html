KEEP THE HOOK IN THE ZONE

hold to charge. hook. reel them in.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Hold anywhere on the water to charge a cast, then release. Longer holds land farther out, where rarer fish feed.
  2. Watch the bobber. When it plunges, tap to hook the fish.
  3. Drag (or use the arrows) to keep the hook inside the moving zone. Stay in to land it. Leave it too long and the line snaps.
  4. Sell fish from the CREEL. Spend the coins in the SHOP on your rod, line, bait and boat.
  5. Fill the LOG. A better boat unlocks new waters, and the time of day changes what bites.
